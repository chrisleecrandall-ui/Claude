Tips for best results:

  • Film from the side view (third-base side for right-handed batters)
  • Keep the camera steady — use a tripod if possible
  • Make sure the full body is visible from feet to bat
  • Good lighting helps — outdoor daylight is ideal
  • Record in slow motion if your phone supports it
  • Upload multiple videos to compare swings side by side
